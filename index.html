Mikiyas Planner Sync

Mikiyas Planner Sync is a personal planning dashboard. It helps its owner, a university student, keep track of tasks, deadlines and messages in one private place so that nothing important is forgotten.

What the app does with Gmail

The app connects to the owner's own Gmail accounts using Google sign-in and requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It reads recent inbox messages and shows the owner a sorted list of mail that needs a reply or attention, such as direct messages from real people, urgent deadlines and security notices. It then lets the owner pin an item to the planner as a task.

It cannot send, delete or change email. It stores only the sender, subject, a short snippet, the date and a link to the message, never full message bodies or attachments.

Who can use it

It is a private tool built and used by one person. It is not a public service and has no other users.
